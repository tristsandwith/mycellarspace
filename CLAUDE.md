# My Cellar Space

A wine fridge tracker app, published as a claude.ai artifact. See `README.md` for the features and the database layout.

- The app source is `my-cellar-space.html`. To publish a change, republish it to the artifact URL in `.claude/skills/wine-research/SKILL.md`, keeping the `db` and `user` capabilities.
- "Research my wines" runs `.claude/skills/wine-research/SKILL.md`.
- The app is built for iPhone Pro: fixed zoom, and form text at 16px or larger.
- Facts only: never invent wine details or tasting notes. Leave a field blank rather than guess.
